# Live Map

The [UniFly Live Map](https://map.unifly.gg/) shows everyone connected to UniFly on a world map, live. It also keeps a
logbook of every flight, hands out badges and runs the leaderboards, events, tours, virtual airlines and stream overlays.

You don't need to do anything to appear on it: connect with the UniFly client and you're on the map within seconds.
Signing in to the map (with Discord) is optional, but it unlocks a few extras like following pilots, saved stream
overlays, flight parties and virtual airlines.

These pages cover what the map can do:

- [Using the map](basics.md): finding your way around, the pilot list, aircraft, airports, weather layers, 3D,
  preferences and keyboard shortcuts.
- [Flights, pilots & badges](flights-pilots.md): the flight panel, timelines, landing rates, logbooks, pilot pages,
  ranks, badges, challenges and leaderboards.
- [Events, tours & community](community.md): events, flight parties, group flights, tours, the destination of the week
  and the UniFly Discord bot.
- [Streaming with UniFly](streaming.md): stream overlays for OBS, showing as live on Twitch, and UniFly Dispatch, our
  Twitch chat bot.
- [Virtual airlines & hangars](airlines-hangars.md): keeping your own aircraft, and starting or joining a virtual
  airline.

> The map is updated often. When a new version comes out a bar at the top of the map offers a reload, and
> **What's new** (in Preferences → About) lists what's changed.

## Your account

Most of the map works without an account. Sign in with Discord from the 👤 button (bottom right) to:

- **Link your pilot.** Fly once with the code your account gives you as your callsign. That proves the pilot is yours.
  If you fly from more than one PC, use **Add another PC** on your account so those flights count as yours too.
- **Follow pilots**, get alerts on every device you sign in on, and have Me mode set up automatically.
- **Make your pilot page your own** with a banner, your Discord picture, home airport, favourite aircraft and links.
- **Save stream overlays** with links that never change.
- **Make a flight party**, ask for an event, keep a hangar and join or start a virtual airline.

Only your Discord ID (kept scrambled) and your name are used, never your email or your servers. Signing up also adds
you to the UniFly Discord (Discord asks first). You can delete your account at any time from its settings.
