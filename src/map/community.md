# Events, tours & community

## Events

The 📅 button lists the group flights and fly-ins coming up: when (in your time), the route, the host and who's going.
The button shows how many new events have been added since you last looked.

- **I'm going** joins an event (Not going leaves it). Clicking *Interested* on the event in the UniFly Discord counts
  too.
- **Copy link** and **📆 Add to calendar** (a file for Outlook, Google or Apple Calendar in your time zone).
- **Repeating events** (weekly, say) show how often they repeat. You say you're going to each one separately.
- **Hide** an event you're not interested in. It's gone from your list and the banner at the top, just for you.
- Route links (SkyVector, SimBrief, Flight Plan Database) in an event's description show as buttons.

From a day before an event until it ends, its route is drawn on the map as a faint dashed line. While it's on, everyone
flying it is picked out in green, has a 🎟️ Event badge in the pilot list, and can be found with the 🎟️ Event filter.
That's anyone who took off from the event's departure or is filed to its arrival, plus anyone who said they're going,
once they're in the air.

**Asking for an event:** signed in, you can ask the UniFly Team for an event of your own. Once they approve it, it's
listed for everyone with you as the host.

**Results:** once an event's over it has results: a podium for the smoothest landings, first there, the quickest and
longest flights, the most flown type, and everyone who flew it. Open 🏆 Results in the Events window for the last 30
days. Sped-up, teleported or slewed landings don't make the podium.

Events have their own badges: *Event pilot*, *Regular attendee*, *Event veteran*, *Made it* (landing at the event's
arrival while it's on) and *Host*.

## Flight parties

Fly with up to 10 friends. Signed in with your pilot linked, make a party from the 👤 menu and share its link or code
for the others to join. You're picked out in teal on each other's maps, with the party's plan (from, to, route and
when) to copy. Whoever runs the party can change the plan. Parties are private: only members see them.

## Group flights

The map spots group flights for you. Three or more pilots taking off from the same airport for the same place within
45 minutes of each other are a group flight, shown in purple with a chip listing who's flying.

## Tours

There are 32 ready-made tours, such as the Alps, Norway's fjords, Route 66, the North Atlantic ferry route, the
Caribbean, Japan and the Himalayas. Find them with 🧭 Tours in the Events window, or *Browse the tours* on a pilot's
Goals tab. Join from your own pilot page and fly the legs in order, at your own pace. There are badges for finishing 1,
5 and 15 tours.

## Destination of the week

Each week (Monday to Sunday, UTC) there's a city or region to fly to, such as London, Tokyo, Cape Town, Reykjavík or
Innsbruck. Its guide lists the airports, a few words about the place, who's been this week, and next week's
destination. The area is shaded on the map. *Hide for this week* tucks it into the 📍 corner button.

Land there during its week (having flown at least 50 nm, from an airport at least 30 nm away) for a **passport stamp**. Stamps add up to the
*Tourist*, *Sightseer*, *Seasoned traveller* and *Well travelled* badges. It isn't a race, so there's no ranking.

## The UniFly Discord

The map has its own bot in the [UniFly Discord](https://unifly.gg/discord):

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/pilot` | A pilot's stats |
| `/flight` | A pilot's flight right now |
| `/whosflying` | Who's in the air |
| `/landings` | Today's best landings |
| `/events` | What's coming up, with links that open each event on the map |
| `/notify` | A DM when a pilot takes off (run it again to stop) |

Names fill in as you type. Every evening the bot posts the **daily highlights** (landing of the day, most time in the
air, the busiest airport, the best badges earned and the day's totals), and the statistics channels show who's flying,
flights today, the best landing today and who's streaming. Pilots who are hidden from the leaderboards or have opted
out are never named.

## Getting help and reporting problems

- **🚩 Report** on a flight (live or in a logbook) sends it to the UniFly Team, for a bad landing rate, a wrong flight
  or anything that looks off.
- If the map seems broken, check the [status page](https://unifly.gg/status) first.
- Everything else: ask in the [UniFly Discord](https://unifly.gg/discord).
